when i was younger, i remember having apprehension from copying what other people were doing. a quick reflection on this leads me to think it's a consequence of trying to distinct oneself in a small tribe (fx. fellow classmates). if we assume this is default behaviour for small tribal groups (if everyone is a berry picker, who is going to hunt?), then it makes sense.

anyway, i think people should the copy each other more. similar to [near](https://near.blog/)'s post, [try a thing](https://near.blog/try-a-thing/), and inspired by the general notion that skillz pay the billz, it's interesting what opportunities arise because of a random skill you saw someone else do, and decided to try yourself.

this could also helps expand your [personality basin](https://near.blog/personality-basins/). if you start doing something which you think you might enjoy, you could potentially end up around people with similar interests, leading to more opportunities.
