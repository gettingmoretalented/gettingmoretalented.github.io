this page lists many of my favorite blog posts, organized by author.

[near.blog](https://near.blog/): really got me into the blog space in general.
 - [links](https://near.blog/links/): various links to other blogs
 - [the future is weird](https://near.blog/weird-future/): weird future
 - [personality basins](https://near.blog/personality-basins/): why are you the way you are
 - [try a thing](https://near.blog/try-a-thing/): you can just do things

[geohot's blog](https://geohot.github.io/blog/): has written down what i always felt to be true. take with a grain of salt. can be summed to: we live in a society, to make society better, produce more than you consume, and don't play zero-sum games.

 - [on money creation](https://geohot.github.io/blog/jekyll/update/2020/08/07/on-money-creation.html): where does value come from?
 - [pieces of shit](https://geohot.github.io/blog/jekyll/update/2021/06/23/pieces-of-shit.html): on zero sum actions in society
 - [money is the map](https://geohot.github.io/blog/jekyll/update/2025/02/24/money-is-the-map.html): not the territory

[tynan](https://tynan.com/): todo, little bit of everything, quite interesting on average, lots of posts
 - [getting into pinball](https://tynan.com/pinball/): on pinball
 - [consistency is key](https://tynan.com/thekey/): most productivity gains can be made from being consistent with the basics
 - [filter more out](https://tynan.com/filter/): on being careful of what you are spending your energy on (i can't wait until ai can do this for us!))
 - [attainable excellence](https://tynan.com/excellence/): basically what every kitchen nightmares episode boils (hah) down to
 - [how to invest in friendships](https://tynan.com/friendvestments/)
 - [positive things about school](https://tynan.com/school/): i think are some good heuristics on how to maximize your school experience
 - [reduce friction](https://tynan.com/reducefriction/): on making life less annoying. smooth is fast, fast is nice.
 - [the benefit of automating everything](https://tynan.com/automateit/)
 - [how to automate your curtains](https://tynan.com/curtains/)

[dynomight](https://dynomight.net): todo
 - [about](https://dynomight.net/about/): interesting take on what to do with your life
 - [better air quality is the easiest way not to die](https://dynomight.net/air/): how simply optimizing your air quality intake leads to high returns on investment for your health
