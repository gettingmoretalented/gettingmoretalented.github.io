thc (the human condition)

created: 02.06.2026 | last updated: 02.06.2026 | status: finished

I read this quote about drugs recently, which really put things into perspective.

You can take all the LSD you want and escape for a while, but the one constant which you will always come back to, is the human condition. You still will get hungry, the sun will still rotate around the Earth, and you still will have to go into work on Monday.

Now replace LSD with any other activity. Still works right?

The one constant in our lives is physics.

I am currently temporarily chill gigging as a gardener, cutting a ~250m long hedge. To be honest, I don’t really care about this hedge. In the future, I will be gone and the hedge will have regrown. What was the point again?

One must imagine Sysephus happy.

This post is really just a wake up call. I get my paycheck, and still sometimes think, what the hell am I going to do with this money anyway? Every passing moment is a battle against entropy, everything around you is slowly decaying, what are you going to do about it?

How many people are working towards sustainably fighting entropy?
