awareness

created: 16.06.2026 | last updated: 16.06.2026 | status: ongoing

i think ever since libraries / books were a widespread thing, it almost didn’t even matter what you have memorized. even more so with the internet, and now, llm’s.

i think the most important thing in this respect is not the knowledge itself, but the meta-knowledge. i think it’s more valueable now than ever to have an awareness in your mind about the state of whats known, and a daydream of what potentially could be.

the problem is that space is huge, and humans’ on average hold about 4 chunks of information at a time.
