copymore

created: 02.06.2026 | last updated: 02.06.2026 | status: ongoing

when i was younger, i remember having apprehension from copying what other people were doing. a quick reflection on this leads me to think it’s a consequence of trying to distinct oneself in a small tribe (fx. fellow classmates). if we assume this is default behaviour for small tribal groups (if everyone is a berry picker, who is going to hunt?), then it makes sense.

anyway, i think people should the copy each other more. similar to near’s post, try a thing, and inspired by the general notion that skillz pay the billz, it’s interesting what opportunities arise because of a random skill you saw someone else do, and decided to try yourself.

this could also helps expand your personality basin. if you start doing something which you think you might enjoy, you could potentially end up around people with similar interests, leading to more opportunities.
