copymore

created: 02.06.2026 | last updated: 07.06.2026 | status: ongoing

when i was younger, i remember having apprehension from copying what other people were doing. a quick reflection on this leads me to think it’s a consequence of trying to distinct oneself in a small tribe (fx. fellow classmates). if everyone is a builder, who is going to hunt for food?

anyway, similar to near’s post, try a thing, and inspired by the general notion that skillz pay the billz, it’s interesting what opportunities arise because of a random skill you saw someone else do, and decided to try yourself.
